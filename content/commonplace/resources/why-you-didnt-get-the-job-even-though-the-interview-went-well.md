An interviewer's perspective on rejections that have nothing to do with how pleasant the interview felt.

## Key ideas
- A weak answer on a core requirement can outweigh brilliant answers on nice-to-haves; ask early which requirements matter most.
- Sometimes someone else was already the favorite, or an internal candidate was the default.
- You can be great and still not fit this particular role (too junior, too senior, or more manager than doer).
- Roles often have hidden requirements the posting never states, like tolerance for no advancement or standing up to a difficult stakeholder.
- With strong competition, the tiebreaker is often something small, like more bonus skills.
- Politeness can hide a bad interview; mock interviews give honest feedback.

> From your list of job requirements, are there any you'd consider especially high priority that I should make sure to highlight during this conversation?
